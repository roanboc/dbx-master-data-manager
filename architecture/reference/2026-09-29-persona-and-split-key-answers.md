# The product owner's answers on a second steward persona and the split's key, 29 September 2026

_[← Reference documents](./README.md)_

A facts-only summary of one conversation with the product owner, on 29 September 2026, while story
3.3 of initiative 3 was reviewed. It records what was asked and answered, in neutral words.

## The questions

1. Locally there is one persona per role, so a batch above 250 decisions takes both steward
   personas, as its maker and its second steward, and neither may answer its blind reviews. How
   should the local mode handle this? Two options were put:
   - a second data-steward persona, "Data steward 2", on a local store only, refused on a shared
     store like every persona;
   - no new persona: locally, such a batch keeps its blind reviews unanswered until initiative 4
     maps people to roles.
2. When a forced-sample review is marked "Not a match", the decide pane asks which comparison misled
   and moves focus to those choices, while the key listener yields to form fields. How should the
   steward confirm? Two options were put:
   - N, or L on another candidate, decides at once while a comparison is chosen, as one exception
     to the listener's rule;
   - the keys stay silent in the choices, and the steward moves to the "Not a match" button.

## The answers

- On the first question, the product owner chose the second data-steward persona, on a local store
  only.
- On the second question, the product owner chose the exception: N, or L on another candidate,
  decides while a comparison is chosen.
